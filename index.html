Questions

Does ModelPenny sit between my app and the AI provider?

No. Your app keeps calling each provider directly. After a call, our SDK sends us the numbers in the background, so a slow or missing ModelPenny never touches your users.

What will ModelPenny store?

Only what your app sends: the provider, the model, the token counts, the time, and the labels you choose. Never your prompts, your answers or your provider keys.

How do the prices stay current?

We read each provider's own pricing page, keep the history of every price, and show the source and when we last checked it, so each call is priced at the rate that was live when you made it.

Can I see the cost per customer or per feature?

Yes. Label each call however you like, such as by customer, feature or team, and see the cost by any of them, across every provider you use.
